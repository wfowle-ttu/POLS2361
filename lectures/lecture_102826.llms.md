# African Aid Dilemma

Africa has recieved 2.6T in foreign aid since the 1960’s. However, most of the countries that have recieved this have ended up poorer or just as poor as before.

## Reasons

Dependency and corruption are listed as some of the most common reasons as to why these countries remain poor. Additionally, cheap donated goods can undercut local producers and damange long run self sufficiency.

Those who receive funds from foreign aid are not easily held accountable. This allows corrupt officials to have strong incentives to meddle with this money. This strengthens corrupt regimes instead of helping.

Therefore, the question is when is foreign aid effective. Under what conditions must foreign aid be given for it to result in strong economic growth and benefits for the recipient country.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

Aid organizations tend to be viewed as corrupt by the populace of recipient nations. These organizations typically have two mandates: 1. Do not give money to corrupt officials. 2. You must spend your entire budget.

Typically, 2 beats 1 in terms of priority as not spending their total budget means that they will have a smaller budget the following year.

# How Foreign Aid Can Be Effective

## Healthcare

This is the sector where the positive impact of foreign aid is most fully documented.

For example, foreign aid has allowed the eradication of several diseases thanks to the spread of medication related to the reduction/eradication of those diseases.

This health aid is typically given to local groups instead of government officials.

## Humanitarian Aid

During wars and famines, foreign aid related to providing food or other types of local assistance can be effective at saving lives and preventing humanitarian disasters.

## Infrastructure Aid

Aid targeted at building roads, bridges, ports, airports, etc. You see China doing this. They do this with ODF (Official Development Finance) recipient countries have to typically pay very high interest loans that are non-defaultable.

## When Aid Tends to Work Best

Aid tends to work best when targeted at specific, measurable problems. When programs are designed with local knowledge and local ownership. Aid should also be monitored and transparent. Aid must also compliment local reform rather than just replacing it. Aid must be delivered in sectors where it can be easily observed, especially areas like health and emergency relief.
